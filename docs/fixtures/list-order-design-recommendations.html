Light Mode

Readable document rhythm with liquid-glass markers.

Ordered Steps

Use compact number chips for process lists, ranked notes, and decision sequences.
  1. Collect sourcesURLs, notes, and screenshots are grouped first.
  2. Rank evidenceMost important item receives a brighter mist rim.
    1. Keep nested depth visible with beads.
    2. Avoid vertical accent rails.
  3. Publish summaryThe list stays compact enough for long documents.

Bullet Lists

Replace flat bullets with small pearl markers and color-coded semantic softness.
  • Deduped contextNeutral aqua pearl for ordinary items.
  • Grounded claimMint marker for reliable, complete items.
    • Nested point keeps a quiet hollow bead.
    • Indent is visible without a left stripe.
  • Ranked priorityWarm marker for attention-worthy items.
