# Prompt: Before Editing

Ask and answer before changing code:

1. Did I read `dev/WIKI/README.md`, `CORE-PRINCIPLES.md`, `QUICK-ROUTING.md`, and the relevant workflow?
2. What surface is this?
3. What is the owner module?
4. Which risk contract applies?
5. Is runtime evidence required?
6. Am I editing the owner or adding a later fix?
7. Which audits must pass?

If any answer is unclear, pause and inspect before editing.
